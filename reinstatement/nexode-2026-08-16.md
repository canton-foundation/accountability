## Applicant Position

* What caused the issue: While performing an internal migration of market data in preparation for Nexode v2, a traffic-usage parameter in the migration tooling was misconfigured, producing bursty, higher-than-usual on-chain activity and burn over roughly a 12-hour window beginning 2026-08-11 UTC. Only transaction/burn volume increased — there was no excess CC reward claim beyond the 115% guidance.
* What has been corrected: The migration was a one-time event and has completed; the feature has been switched off and its configuration corrected and bounded so the same misconfiguration cannot recur. The underlying issues in Nexode's internal services have been fixed, rate limits are now in place to prevent high traffic usage, and monitoring has been added to alert on anomalous traffic/burn patterns.
* Date corrective measures took effect: On or about 2026-08-11 UTC, within hours of the activity being flagged. The feature has remained off since.
