## About Tabiri Markets

Tabiri Market is a prediction market platform where users trade on the outcomes of real-world events. Users buy and sell shares representing event probabilities (YES/NO outcomes), with prices reflecting market consensus.

The platform uses an automated LMSR (Logarithmic Market Scoring Rule) market maker for instant liquidity and processes all trades with instant on-chain settlement via Canton Network.

## Reviewer Notes

**Product activity and fee model.** Tabiri’s core activity is prediction-market betting: users place bets and Tabiri charges a fee on each bet. Users may also batch multiple bets into a single submission to reduce per-bet network fees. The application exposes an API for programmatic access.

**Swap feature (negligible traffic).** Tabiri includes a swap feature, but analytics show it is barely used. It was added only because tf-USDT was not available elsewhere on the network and many users needed a way to convert CC into tf-USDT for trading. Swap-related traffic has been effectively negligible relative to betting activity.

**Early joining bonus.** Early on, Tabiri offered a joining bonus of 10,000 tf-USDT to new users. The bonus was locked for the first 30 days (could not be withdrawn off-platform) and required the user to place at least one bet, so the grant was tied to actual product use rather than unconstrained free distribution of assets.

**Primary settlement asset.** Tabiri’s top / primary asset is **tf-USDT**, not the TradeFast test token. Tabiri chose tf-USDT because a USD-backed stablecoin is the natural unit of account for a prediction market.
