## 1. Root Cause

Every validator on the Global Synchronizer receives a free base-rate traffic allowance, which is
consumed before any purchased traffic.

From 1 to 29 October 2026 (UTC), our transactions on the validators involved used only that free
allowance, and we did not purchase traffic to match that consumption. This produced the pattern the
committee identified: traffic consumed without a matching purchase. We do not dispute the
committee's figures for that period.

---

## 2. Corrective Actions

1. **Traffic purchases.** We are changing our transaction pattern so that the validators involved
   purchase traffic instead of relying only on the free allowance.

   * In effect since: 1 November 2026
   * Evidence: every traffic purchase by each validator involved (date, traffic bought, CC paid and
     transaction update ID), recorded by our traffic monitor from 1 November 2026.

2. **Tokenomic equilibrium.** We maintain the network's tokenomic equilibrium. The traffic our
   validators use is paid for through traffic purchases that burn CC, so what we take from the
   network is balanced by what we return to it.

3. **Real retail growth.** Our focus is authentic retail adoption: activity on Grofty should reflect
   real users, real needs and real value.

---

## 3. Ongoing Monitoring

* **Automatic traffic purchases.** Traffic is purchased automatically by the validators, without
  depending on manual top-ups.
* **Traffic monitoring.** Since 2 November 2026 we have monitored the traffic consumption and purchases
  of every validator involved, from on-chain data.
* Grofty consents to a follow-up review three months after reinstatement (see Basis for Continued
  Compliance).

## Basis for Continued Compliance

**The function Grofty serves for the network and its users.** Grofty Wallet is a non-custodial
Canton wallet. Users hold, send and receive CC, USDCx and other CIP-56/CIP-0112 tokens, swap between
them inside the wallet, bridge in from Ethereum, Base and Monad, and connect to Canton dApps through
CIP-0103. Transfer
preapprovals and automatic UTXO merging are set up for every wallet. We also run Grofty Lens
(https://lens.grofty.cc/), a public market-data service for the Canton ecosystem.

**Grofty in the Canton ecosystem.** Grofty is a member of the Canton Foundation, and we have secured
our licence and MSA with Digital Asset to become an issuer on the DA Registry. We are working with
issuers on DvP and pools; this work is about 65% complete and is in its testing, stability and
security phase.

**The rule Grofty now operates under.** We make sure the validators involved purchase traffic for
what they use, and every traffic purchase burns CC.

**Follow-up review.** Grofty consents to a follow-up review three months after reinstatement,
covering the first full quarter, with a summary submitted to the Tokenomics and Accountability
committees.
