## 1. Root Cause

The flagged traffic came from CBTC transfers between user parties hosted on validators operated by
our partners. The transfers were initiated by those partners as part of the activity they ran on the
validators they operate, which generated the BitSafe revenue share that is Grofty's income.

Those validators ran that activity on the synchronizer's free base-rate allowance and did not
purchase traffic to match it. We did not treat the free allowance as something that had to be matched
by purchases; this was wrong. The Featured App Guidance v2.0b, published on 28 September 2026, states
that there is no elasticity for free traffic, and the activity nevertheless continued through October
and, at a reduced pace, until 5 November. We take responsibility for not applying the guidance when
it was published.

We do not dispute the committee's figures. The validators involved, their operators, and the date
each one started buying traffic are in the validator list linked in section 2, together with a link
to the purchase records. The rewards associated with this activity will be burned by BitSafe.

---

## 2. Corrective Actions

1. **Traffic purchases, rolled out from 31 October.** After the pause announcement on 30 October
   2026, the validators involved started buying traffic in stages: 5 validators on 31 October,
   10 more on 2 November, and 6 more on 3 November, 21 validators in total by 5 November
   (104 purchases, 21.2 MB, USD 1,272).

   * List of the validators involved (party ID, operator, date active, date it started buying
     traffic): [validator list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MCaAcVK7GCKYZn3PYHv4Xn0vFuokPvof/edit?usp=sharing)
   * Evidence: every traffic purchase by each validator involved since 30 October 2026 (date,
     traffic bought, CC paid and transaction update ID), recorded by our traffic monitor:
     [traffic purchase data](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14d1U7515CNW36pDw5CgD0XqOeCQDc2NP/edit?usp=sharing)

2. **Activity stopped on 6 November.** We stopped the CBTC transfer activity on the validators
   involved on 6 November 2026. Since then the validators only submit their own liveness
   transactions (about 45 MB per day across all of them, against about 1,130 MB per day before),
   and the activity will not be resumed on the free allowance.

3. **Going forward.** Any traffic used by these validators is paid for through traffic purchases
   that burn CC, bought before the traffic is used, so what is taken from the network is matched by
   what is returned to it. The rewards associated with the flagged activity are being burned by
   BitSafe (section 5).

## Basis for Continued Compliance

**The function Grofty serves for the network and its users.** Grofty Wallet is a non-custodial
Canton wallet. Users hold, send and receive CC, USDCx and other CIP-56/CIP-0112 tokens, swap between
them inside the wallet, bridge in from Ethereum, Base and Monad, and connect to Canton dApps through
CIP-0103. Transfer
preapprovals and automatic UTXO merging are set up for every wallet. We also run Grofty Lens
(https://lens.grofty.cc/), a public market-data service for the Canton ecosystem.

**Grofty in the Canton ecosystem.** Grofty is a member of the Canton Foundation, and we have secured
our licence and MSA with Digital Asset to become an issuer on the DA Registry. We are working with
issuers on DvP and pools; this work is about 65% complete and is in its testing, stability and
security phase.

**The rule Grofty now operates under.** We make sure the validators involved purchase traffic for
what they use, and every traffic purchase burns CC.

**Follow-up review.** Grofty consents to a follow-up review three months after reinstatement,
covering the first full quarter, with a summary submitted to the Tokenomics and Accountability
committees.
